Shared structure

Every mock is built the same way so the differences you see are the decisions below, not accidents.

Idle body Abstract glowing HUD orb: concentric rings, slow pulse, one orbiting mote.
Active body The orb resolves into a humanoid bust with glowing eyes, a chest core and two HUD panels; the orb recedes behind it. Speaking adds a pulse ring, a moving mouth and live panel bars.
Card view Roster tile: idle orb, name, role line, status and the last line of the chat.
Chat view Large body with name, a transcript and a composer. Mobile stacks it; desktop puts body and chat side by side (layout A) or under a band (layout B).
Tokens Colour, type and the two body tones are named custom properties per mock; dark is the designed default plus a designed light set.
Motion Transform and opacity only. Reduced motion turns animation off; idle (orb) and active (bust) still look different.

What to choose

  1. (a) Body treatment. Orb-first (the roster is a field of calm glowing orbs and a bust only appears when an agent speaks, as in these mocks) or bust-first (the figure is always visible, the orb is only a loading or away state). Open any mock and switch to Active to judge how much the bust should lead.
  2. (b) Layout. A grid of tiles that opens a full chat, or a persistent side rail beside the chat. Compare Hephaestus layout A with Hephaestus layout B; the choice then applies to all agents.
  3. (c) Overall mood. Warm and tactile (forge), cool and precise (gate), or calm and bright (sun). Each agent keeps its own palette either way; this decides how loud the shared chrome is.
